## The Core Insight

The single most important (and most overlooked) design decision in electronic access control is **fail-safe vs fail-secure** — what the door does when power is cut:

- **Fail-safe** (unlock on power loss) → egress / fire routes
- **Fail-secure** (stay locked on power loss) → high-security areas

Maglocks are inherently fail-safe (electromagnetic); electric strikes can be configured either way. Get this wrong and you create a life-safety violation.
