## A Note on Accuracy

Licensing lists **change**. Some states sunset licenses (Illinois plans 2029), some cities reform (NYC 2027). We mark source dates and flag the "13 vs 15 states" discrepancy honestly rather than presenting a frozen, potentially wrong list.

## How This Hub Works

This hub is E-E-A-T leverage: it helps people enter the trade and understand credentialing. We distinguish **license** (legal requirement to operate) from **certification** (professional signal), which is the most common confusion in this space.
