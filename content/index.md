# Locktool — Professional Locksmith Tools & Lock Technology Resources

**Knowledge-first resource for locksmiths, security technicians, and lock technology professionals.**

Locktool is a knowledge ecosystem built around a simple premise: **the lock industry deserves authoritative, technically rigorous, safety-first information — not thin affiliate content.**

This knowledge base is organized into six hubs, each with a distinct purpose and independent topic coverage:

| Hub | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Lock Technology** | How lock mechanisms actually work — pin tumbler, wafer, disc detainer, dimple, tubular, lever. |
| **Tools & Equipment** | What locksmith tools exist, how they're classified, and how to select them. |
| **Certification & Training** | How to enter the trade, certification levels, and licensing by jurisdiction. |
| **Standards & Compliance** | ANSI/BHMA, EN 1303, UL ratings — how to read and compare security standards. |
| **Vault & Safe** | Safe lock types and burglary ratings, decoded. |
| **Access Control** | Electronic locks, electric strikes, maglocks, and fail-safe/fail-secure design. |

## Our Position on Responsible Use

Locktool is for **licensed, authorized, and professional use**. Our content explains *how locks work* and *how to evaluate them* — it does **not** teach bypass, defeat, or unauthorized entry techniques.

Every article carries:
- **`## The Author's Take`** — a first-person editorial conclusion with a clear stance and disclosure of what is fact vs. professional judgment.
- **Authoritative sources** — government, standards organizations, and manufacturer documentation, never fabricated data.
- **Responsible-use framing** where tool categories carry legal or safety sensitivity.

## Who This Is For

- Licensed locksmiths and lock service technicians
- Security integrators and access-control professionals
- Facility managers evaluating hardware
- Students and apprentices entering the trade
- Anyone who wants honest, technical, non-sensational lock information
