## The Core Insight

Lock standards **are not directly comparable across regions**. ANSI Grade measures durability + forced-entry resistance; EN 1303 is an 8-digit per-dimension code; UL 437 measures attack-resistance time — and none of them fully define "high security."

We actively correct the common mythologies (e.g., "UL 437 = high security lock," "Grade 1 = pick-proof").

## Note on Legal Information

Compliance content cites government and standards bodies, with source dates. It is **information, not legal advice** — always verify current regulations in your jurisdiction before relying on anything here.
