## The Core Insight

Lock standards **are not directly comparable across regions**. ANSI Grade measures durability + forced-entry resistance; EN 1303 is an 8-digit per-dimension code; TS007 patches EN 1303's snap blind spot; UL 437 measures attack-resistance minutes — and none of them fully define "high security."

Each article turns the "decode" into an **engineering parameter matrix**: grading → test method → threshold → application. Read the grade as a *procurement signal* (what threat it covers, what to buy), not a headline number.

We actively correct the common mythologies (e.g., "UL 437 = high security lock," "Grade 1 = pick-proof," "fire exit = panic hardware").

## Note on Legal Information

Compliance content cites government and standards bodies, with source dates. It is **information, not legal advice** — always verify current regulations in your jurisdiction before relying on anything here.
