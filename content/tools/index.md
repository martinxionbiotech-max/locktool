## Responsible Use

Some categories here (lock picks, tension tools, decoders) carry legal sensitivity in certain jurisdictions. We classify tools by risk grade (A/B/C) per our Legal Risk Matrix, and frame B/C-class tools with responsible-use context — never bypass instruction.

## How This Hub Works

This is the closest hub to commerce: tool knowledge naturally links to product selection. But we prioritize **honest evaluation** over selling — including calling out when a tool is overrated (e.g., tubular picks) or has hidden costs (e.g., automotive programmer subscriptions).
