## The Core Insight

Two **separate** rating systems apply to a safe, and conflating them is the most common buyer mistake:

- **Lock rating** (UL 768 Groups / UL 2058 Type 1) — resistance to *manipulation* (dial-reading, X-ray).
- **Body rating** (UL 687 RSC/TL/TRTL) — resistance to *physical forced entry* (prying, drilling, torching).

A top-tier lock on a thin-steel body is still a weak safe.
